# House Wayfire

House Wayfire was the noble family of [[John Wayfire]], the cis patriarch whose name became a war. The house carried out the [[First Wayfire]] and went on to govern the [[Wayfire (Province)]], the region they came to inhabit. The house took its name from the conflict, which proved highly profitable.

In [[Calanthiad|The Calanthiad]], Calantha cuts down every adult of House Wayfire she finds while rescuing the trans femmes held in Wayfire (Province), sparing only the children.
