# Wayfire (Province)

Wayfire (Province) is the rural northern region of the old order. It was first settled by the [[Valeran]] people, who colonized it after fleeing the [[Vaeloran Coalition]] before the [[First Wayfire]] and brought their seafaring culture; at that time it was known as the Duchy of Wayfire, separated from the Wayfires by a plateau that offered geographic barriers against the encroaching urbanized south.

After the First Wayfire, the noble family [[House Wayfire]] came to inhabit and govern the region, which took its name from the conflict. Trans femmes were held there under the Ancien regime.
