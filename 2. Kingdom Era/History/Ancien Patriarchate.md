The Ancien Patriarchate was the overarching government of the island before the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]], the cis-led regime that oppressed the trans-led peoples. It is to Ancien what the Roman Empire was to Rome: the empire, not the place.

The Patriarchate came later than the land it ruled. [[Ancien]], the homeland, was united first, growing out of the city of [[New Columbia]]. Under the feudal system that followed, aristocratic families arose, and it was from those families that the patriarchate was formed. The way the Roman Republic gave way to the Roman Empire, the patriarchal government formed only after the land was centralized. Under that government the realm expanded outward into new territory, but it kept the name Ancien rather than replacing the homeland's identity. The [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]] overthrew the Patriarchate and ended Ancien rule, clearing the way for [[Luminara]].
