Veterans self-identify as such. They chose their names in reference to their martial past, but mostly out of a desire to find a more peaceful life. To a Luminaran, a veteran is someone who wants to settle down after a time of war and hurt. The Veterans look back to [[Calantha]] as a role model for how they would like to live their lives, settling a new land and living in peace, only this time they are able to.
