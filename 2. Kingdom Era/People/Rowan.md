> Rowan, a butch knight from [[Bloodmarch]], stands as a symbol of strength and loyalty within the ranks of the revolutionary leadership.

Rowan served as the lover, bodyguard, and captain of the heavy infantry under Seraphina during the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]]. Despite her lack of political acumen, her unwavering faithfulness to the Princess and her deep devotion to her serve as her greatest strengths. Her service was not without its trials; before the war, Rowan spent years defending the frontlines of the disparate Bloodmarch tribes, particularly the Veterans, who were the most ravaged of all trans tribes. The Bloodmarchers aimed to exterminate the Veterans first, and their attacks were relentless and merciless. These years left Rowan scarred both physically and emotionally, but they also forged a resilience that defines her as a leader.

The Province of Rowan, a valley frontier where soldiers retire in safety, is named in her honor. This recognition speaks to her importance within the revolutionary movement, but it also serves as a testament to the enduring impact of her service and the respect she commands among her peers.

Rowan's strength and dedication continue to inspire those around her, making her an irreplaceable part of the revolutionary leadership in the Luminara world.
