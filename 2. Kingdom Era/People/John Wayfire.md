> The cis patriarch whose name became a war.

John Wayfire was a cis man of the [[Ancien]] empire and the familial patriarch of his house, its head, not the ruler of Ancien itself. He sought to wage a colonial war against the trans-led peoples of the island.

The conflict he set in motion became known as the [[First Wayfire]], and he took his surname from the act. The name is an eponym earned through atrocity, akin to being called Adolf von Holocaust. The noble family [[House Wayfire]] carried out the Wayfire and went on to govern the [[Wayfire (Province)]], the region they came to inhabit. The house took its name from the conflict, which proved highly profitable.

John Wayfire is the enemy patriarch of the [[Calanthiad]], the war the trans femme heroine Calantha opposed. In her reckoning she cut down the adult generation of [[House Wayfire]] and rescued the trans femmes held in the [[Wayfire (Province)]], sparing only the children.
