Ancien is the region at the heart of the old order, the place where power lay. Think of it as Rome in the sense of Rome the city and its surrounding heartland: a place, not the government that ruled from it. Its capital city is [[New Columbia]].

The name means "land of the ancient ones." The realm grew from a single city: [[New Columbia]] began as a city, and the land they united around it became Ancien. Out of that united land a feudal system arose, and from it the aristocratic families who would form the [[Ancien Patriarchate]].

Before it was the [[Ancien Patriarchate]], it was simply called Ancien. The parallel is Gaul and Francia: a homeland first, then a government that took its name from that homeland even as its borders changed. The people conquered and centralized Ancien before they developed the patriarchate, the way the Roman Republic preceded the Roman Empire. The patriarchate then expanded outward, keeping the name Ancien for the whole realm even as it swallowed lands beyond the original home territory.
