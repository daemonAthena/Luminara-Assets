# Valeran

The Valerans were a people who fled the [[Vaeloran Coalition]] before the [[First Wayfire]]. Exiled Provincial Luminarans from the [[First Wayfire]], they are the direct descendants of the original settlers of Luminara. They colonized the rural north of what was then known as the [[Wayfire (Province)|Duchy of Wayfire]] and brought with them their seafaring culture.

After their cities were destroyed they were the first to flee to the countryside, but kept their walled communities instead of adopting the [[Traveling Camp]]. They were separated from the Wayfires by a plateau offering geographic barriers to the encroaching urbanized south.

They were integral in the blockading efforts during the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]] and remained a separate and distinct culture even when their ancestral homelands were recolonized by a variety of trans people.
