> The golden haired of the city, who coordinated the rebellion's fleets and led with a Hamiltonian energy.

Aurelia was one of the two most trusted lieutenants and secretaries of [[Seraphina]] during the [[Blooming War]], the other being [[Elowynn]] of the countryside. She was from the city of [[Eos]], the trade heart of the island, named for the light god. She took her name for her golden hair.

## Early Life
Raised under [[Ancien]] rule, Aurelia was sheltered by a loving mother, though Eos and its surrounding region of [[Greatmill]] were comparatively tolerant of trans women. Even so, she had to boymode or pass to avoid harm.

## Role in the Blooming War
Aurelia defected to the rebellion and served as its expert coordinator, managing logistics, the navy, and affairs of state. Described as a gifted kid, she carried a Hamiltonian energy to the war effort.
