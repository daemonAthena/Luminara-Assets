Splorin Florin is an expert whose duties are considered sacred; her job is to press flowers into the money (the Flower currency). She is an ordinary girl who picks her next successor based on who passes her apprenticeship. Her apprentice takes on her last name and does the job next. She likes picking new flowers to print into the money and has very few worries in life. She comes from a [[Doll]] family in [[Luminaris (Province)|Luminaris]] and frequently sails to [[Province of Aurelia|Aurelia]] to visit the prismatic field where all the different flowers on the island grow at once. Once, she met the [[Hermit Prince]] wandering through the fields quietly and gave him a flower. The Prince blushed and wrote to [[Seraphina]] about it. Seraphina thought it was cute, so she had some folks pick a bunch of flowers and pre-press them to save Florin some time as a nice present. The actual printing of the bills is handled by a more advanced technological infrastructure set up by the technocratic folks in [[Vaelora]].
