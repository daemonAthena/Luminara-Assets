# Bloodmarch

Bloodmarch is a region of rolling hills, the homeland of the [[Veteran]] culture and the site of cruel Ancien policies toward trans women. The Veterans, descendants of the heavy fighters of the [[Blooming War]], were the most victimized and traumatized of the trans cultures due to Bloodmarch's treatment, and many live with PTSD. The [[Fidelans]] sheltered any Veteran who found herself south of the Bloodmarch hills.

During the Blooming War, Luminara defeated Bloodmarch through the Bloodmarch campaign, using propaganda to turn its lords against their rulers after a series of pitched battles fought uphill.
