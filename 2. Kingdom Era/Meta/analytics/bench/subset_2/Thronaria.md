# Thronaria

Thronaria is an eastern capital of Luminara, where Hellenism is very popular. In the Ancien Patriarchate it was the Crownlands, home to the Grey Lord. It was the site of [[The Siege of Thronaria]], the final battle of the [[Blooming War]], where its walls were broken down and the Grey Lord abdicated to [[Seraphina]].
