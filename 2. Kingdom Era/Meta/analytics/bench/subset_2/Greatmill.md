# Greatmill

Greatmill was a region of the [[Ancien Patriarchate]] containing the city of [[Eos]] and surrounding lands comparatively tolerant of trans women. During the [[Blooming War]], Luminara provoked instability among House Graci's vassals, then took the province in the war's second year. For their passivity the lives of the Graci were spared, and ambitious vassals were rewarded with land in exchange for greater trans governance.
