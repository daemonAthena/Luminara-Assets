---

Dolls fundamentally uplift each other. They see each other as wanderers in this world, showing each other love as if they were sisters. Their bonds are the closest, and they take the most pride in their identities. They mostly want to encourage a maternal society that cares for each new addition and helps guide them to find their way. They see themselves as noble not because they are better but because they wish to embody their ideals. They tip their hats to each other as nobility.

As far as non-Dolls go, they do not see them as worse. They see non-Dolls as kin who have different ways. Most Dolls live along the Estroveil river, a new network of cities built above the ruins of the old Vaeloran Coalition. Splorin Florin comes from a Doll family in Luminaris.
