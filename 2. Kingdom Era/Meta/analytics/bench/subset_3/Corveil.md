Corveil, a bustling border town, stands as a testament to the complex intermingling of Luminaran and Ancien cultures. Nestled between the rolling hills and the vast, turbulent sea, Corveil has become a haven for refugees from both sides of the conflict against the Ancien Patriarchate. The town's strategic location, situated just a day's sail from the coast and within easy reach of the interior, has made it a natural point of exchange for goods, ideas, and people.

The streets of Corveil are a vibrant tapestry of colors and scents, with Luminaran artisans and merchants sharing spaces with Ancien traders and craftsmen. Markets overflow with exotic goods—spices from distant lands, rare textiles, and unique wares that reflect the diverse backgrounds of its inhabitants. Despite the close proximity and shared history, tensions occasionally flare, particularly among the older generations who remember the strife and struggle of the past. However, the younger residents of Corveil have grown up in a world where these differences are seen as opportunities for mutual understanding and prosperity.
