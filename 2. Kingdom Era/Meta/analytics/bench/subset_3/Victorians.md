# Victorians

Victorians reside in the [[Province of Elowynn]]. They are the descendants of rural trans femmes who managed to find some semblance of peace and open society in the rural forests and plains of the province known as Vichi during their diaspora. They were the bulk of the Luminaran army during the [[Blooming War]]. As such they call themselves after victory. They grew out of the [[Artemai]], the once nomadic [[Traveling Camp]] who after the [[Blooming War]] settled into the rural life they had always yearned for.

"Vichi" is the name cis colonizers gave the region. Those colonizers did take root there, but they failed to press into its heartland, for the waters were treacherous. They laid claim to all of it on paper, yet the Victorians already existed quietly in the valleys between [[Ancien]] and [[Bloodmarch]].
