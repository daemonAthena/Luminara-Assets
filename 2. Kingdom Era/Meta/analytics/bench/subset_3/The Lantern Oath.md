The Lantern Oath is an esteemed order of artisan-scholars based in the ancient lighthouse archives of Artemai, a coastal city renowned for its strategic importance and rich history. The order was established following the revolution against the Ancien Patriarchate, when the trans women leaders recognized the critical need to preserve the knowledge and heritage of their people. The Lantern Oath is tasked with safeguarding and disseminating historical records, maps, and technical knowledge that are crucial for the advancement and defense of the new world order.

Members of the Lantern Oath are trained in both the practical arts of navigation and construction as well as in the scholarly pursuits of history and philosophy. They are responsible for maintaining the lighthouse archives, which house a vast collection of documents and artifacts that trace the history of Artemai and the broader struggles of the revolution. The order operates under the strictest confidentiality, ensuring that the secrets and strategies of the past are protected for the benefit of the present and future generations.
