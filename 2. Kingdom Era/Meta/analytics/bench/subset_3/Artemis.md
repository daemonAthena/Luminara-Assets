# Artemis

Artemis is the goddess of the hunt, woodcarving, defense, femininity, polyamory, and carnal love.

She is a trans-feminine reimagining of the Hellenic Artemis: the classical "virgin" aspect is read here not as chastity but as youthful, energetic, independent spirit. The virginity is dropped; the wild self-sovereignty remains.

She is the matron deity of the [[Artemai]]: the people trace their name and lineage to her, counting themselves among her line through the trans adoption of [[Calantha]].
