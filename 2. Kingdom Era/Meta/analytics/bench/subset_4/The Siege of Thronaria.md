# The Siege of Thronaria

The Siege of Thronaria was the final battle of the [[Blooming War]]. After the collapse of the major houses of Ancien, Luminaran forces broke down the walls of Thronaria and, at swordpoint, forced the Grey Lord to abdicate to [[Seraphina]], ending the war in a Luminaran victory.
