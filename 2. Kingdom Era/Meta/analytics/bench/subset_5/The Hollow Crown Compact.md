The Hollow Crown Compact was a clandestine agreement between several minor provinces in Luminara, primarily located along the eastern coast. Formed in the aftermath of the Great Uprising against the Ancien Patriarchate, this pact was designed to ensure that member provinces could share grain reserves during sieges. The compact was necessary due to the frequent blockades and attacks by the remnants of the old regime, which often left coastal provinces in desperate need of food supplies.

Despite its secretive nature, the Hollow Crown Compact was instrumental in maintaining the stability of these provinces, allowing them to support their populations through lean times. The agreement was kept under strict confidentiality, with only a select few within each province privy to its details. The compact relied on trust and mutual benefit, with provinces agreeing to open their granaries to others in exchange for similar promises of support. This shared resource helped to strengthen the unity and resilience of these coastal communities, contributing to the overall resistance against the Ancien Patriarchate.
