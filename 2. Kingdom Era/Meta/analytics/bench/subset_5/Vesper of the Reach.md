The Vesper of the Reach is a revered figure among the river settlements of the coastal cities. This wandering mediator is known for her impartial and wise judgment, often called upon to resolve conflicts that arise between neighboring settlements. Her name is whispered with respect as she travels from town to town, her presence a sign of hope for peaceful resolutions. The Vesper of the Reach is not just a human; she is a symbol of the unity and cooperation that the trans women of the revolution sought to foster. Her mediations are based on ancient customs and modern principles alike, ensuring that justice is served without the biases that have historically plagued the Ancien Patriarchate. Despite her travels, the Vesper of the Reach maintains a deep connection to the waterways, drawing her strength from the rivers themselves and guiding her work with a gentle but resolute spirit.
