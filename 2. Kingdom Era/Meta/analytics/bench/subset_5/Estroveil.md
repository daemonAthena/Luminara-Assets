> The river of our becoming

The river is the largest on the island with passive waters fit for speedy travel up and down stream and home to some of its largest cities.

It spans multiple biomes and elevations cultivating a rich trade network across the region and connecting the center of the province with its south.

The disparity of connectivity between the two parts of Luminara is so great that Luminarans survived in exile north of the river for 3 generations and resettled the region after [[History of the Blooming War|The Blooming War]].
