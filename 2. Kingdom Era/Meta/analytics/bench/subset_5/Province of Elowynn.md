# Province of Elowynn

The Province of Elowynn (also linked as [[Province of Elowynn|Elowynn (Province)]]) is the rural province named for [[Elowynn]], a leader who fled the [[Vaeloran Coalition]] before the [[First Wayfire]] and lived as a contemporary of [[Seraphina]]. The naming follows the pattern of U.S. states named after Founding Fathers.

It was known as Vichi under cis colonizers. The [[Victorians]] descend from rural trans femmes who found peace there during their diaspora, and formed the bulk of the Luminaran army in the [[Blooming War]].
