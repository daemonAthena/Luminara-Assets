> The Exconventica, a resilient and vibrant community, emerged from the ruins of the convent system in [[Thronaria]], the old capital region of [[Ancien]]. Freed from the confines of the convents after the [[Blooming War]], these trans women transformed [[Thronaria]] into a queer sanctuary, reshaping it from the shadows of the past into a beacon of love and healing.

The Exconventica were once among the countless trans women who were taken from their homes across Ancien for "retraining." These convents, scattered around Thronaria, were not merely places of learning but sites of coercion and indoctrination. The trans women were taught to suppress their true selves, forced to adopt the identity of males within the convent walls.

When the Luminaran government granted them freedom, the cities of Thronaria saw a dramatic shift. The Exconventica were given the right to live peacefully in neighborhoods across the region, a step toward healing and reclaiming their identities. They rebuilt Thronaria, infusing it with a rich nightlife and a culture that celebrated love over the few churches that remained. They focus on healing alongside each other in unbound acts of love.

Their symbol, the nun's habit, is a powerful and deliberate statement. This garment, once a symbol of oppression, now represents a defiant embrace of their true selves. The Exconventica wear the habit with irony and defiance, often transforming it into a risque fashion statement. They use it as a canvas for drag, reclaiming it as a symbol of their resilience.

The Exconventica are not a distinct tribal people but a shared history. They come from diverse backgrounds, some from cultures that never tolerated transness, others rounded up from the borders of [[Artemai]] and [[Valeran]]. Despite their varied origins, they share a common experience: the indoctrination and coerced labor within the convents. This shared trauma forged a deep sense of connection among them.

Today, Thronaria stands as a testament to the resilience of the Exconventica. It is a place where they live, love, and heal, celebrating their unique heritage and the strength that comes from their shared history.
