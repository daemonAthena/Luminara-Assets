> The Mother of the gods, cast down into the earth, who dictates the cycle of life from below.

Neos is the goddess of life and death, harvest, the sun, butterflies (metamorphosis), and growth. She is the Mother of the gods.

She was overthrown and cast down into the earth, where she dictates the cycle of life. The gods opposed her tyranny and fought to free themselves from it, and that is how they became immortal.
