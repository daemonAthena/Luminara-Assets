# Design

Once the invisibility is deactivated it illuminates them making their presence known to everyone closeby. The limitations of this artifact are unclear. It appears, however, that when the user becomes exhausted they curl up on the floor and remain docile until they regain their stamina. It appears wearing the dress is quite draining.
