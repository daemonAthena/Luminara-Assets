# Terminology

There exists a device known by the old vixens of the country as the frollo trap.

# History

It is attached to many entryways to barns and sheds in the countryside which we believe to have housed pre blooming illicit activities.

We've found the decaying remains of men and women alike who've fallen into this trap.

# Design

Upon being triggered by the opening of the door the trap springs a knife into the back of the opener, hitting them in the spine thus freezing them in place, and then pulls them beneath the floorboards to eternally rest.
