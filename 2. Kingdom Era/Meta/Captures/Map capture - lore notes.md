- [[Vespera]]: The feral huntress city of Vespera lies deep in the wild woodlands upstream from Luminara. Home to the coalition's most skilled trackers and archers, Vespera is less a traditional city and more a collection of lodges and tree platforms woven into the forest itself. Its people embrace a wild, untamed way of life  -  fluid, expressive, and deeply connected to the land. They move freely between the settlement and the deep woods, following the rhythms of the hunt. Revered for their self-reliance and deadly accuracy, the Vesperans supply the coalition with meat, fine furs, leather, and elite warriors.

- [[Vaelora]]: Perched dramatically atop high cliffs overlooking the mouth of the Estravel River, Vaelora serves as the undisputed economic heart of the Valoran coalition. Its position at the river's end, where the waters plunge over the cliffs in a great waterfall, forces all trade to pass through its ingenious cliffside cargo lift system. This natural choke point made Vaelora extraordinarily wealthy. Known for its cosmopolitan outlook, maritime culture, and shrewd merchants, Vaelora controlled all external trade for the coalition and served as its primary gateway to the outside world.

- [[Sicklesheaf]]: Located in the fertile grasslands along a major tributary, Sickle Sheaf is the primary agricultural heartland of the Valoran coalition. This sprawling settlement began as a collection of large farming estates and grew into a major town that feeds much of the river system. It is known for its vast wheat fields, cattle herds, and massive granaries. The people of Sickle Sheaf are practical, generous, and deeply tied to the seasonal rhythms of planting and harvest. Their grain, flour, and livestock are shipped downriver to Luminara for processing and to Vaelora for export.

- [[Dikereth]]: DikerethNestled in the rugged, mineral-rich hills upstream from Luminara, Dikereth is the coalition's hardy industrial heart. It began as a mining settlement on the slopes of Mt Modelia, where the coal seams break the surface, and grew fat on the industrial revolution: the mines at the mountain's foot, the High Crag terrace and the Southface colliery now feed a ring of bloomeries, forges and workshops that smelt ore and turn out iron tools, nails, blades and simple weapons rather than shipping heavy raw metal downriver.Coal made the town. Once the easy seams were worked out the workings climbed the slopes, and the freight railway came to carry coal and coke down to the forges and finished iron away to the coast. Smoke rises constantly over the town and the ringing of hammers is a constant sound; the people are blunt, hardworking and no-nonsense, proud of their role as the coalition's primary producers of practical, durable goods.
