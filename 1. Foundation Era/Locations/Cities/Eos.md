# Eos

Eos is the trade heart of the island and the city at the center of the [[Province of Aurelia]]. Named for the light god, it was the home of [[Aurelia]], the golden-haired lieutenant of [[Seraphina]] during the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]]. Under Ancien rule, Eos and the surrounding region of [[Greatmill]] were comparatively tolerant of trans women.
