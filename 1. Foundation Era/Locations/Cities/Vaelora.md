> The place where the sea learns to fly

Vaelora is a port city at the mouth of the [[Estroveil River]] river and an early [[Vaeloran Coalition|Coalition City]].

It is situated on a cliffside with a waterfall overlooking a sheltered cove where a port was constructed.

It is a major trade hub featuring a waterfall spilling out into the ocean. This waterfall acts as a barrier to traversing the river estroveil up and downstream.

Multiple large platforms, pullies, and counterweights have been hollowed into the cliffside to make the unloading of cargo happen quick. The creak of ropes mimicking the heartbeat of the city. 

There is an upper port up the river on the cliff and a lower port down the falls at the bay.

Vaelora serves as the perfect bottleneck for defense against naval attacks on Luminara.
