# City of Luminaris

> A city of converging rivers and crafted goods, Luminaris stands where the Estroveil forks meet.

City of Luminaris is a major urban center in the Province of Luminaris, situated at the confluence of the two main branches of the [[Estroveil River]] river. Unlike [[Vaelora]], which sits at the river's mouth, Luminaris occupies the upstream point where the eastern and western branches of the river curve around the city's eastern and western edges, creating natural defensive barriers on two sides.

## Geography and Layout

The city is positioned at the single point of convergence between the two forking branches of the Estroveil. This strategic location makes Luminaris both highly defensible and a crucial trade hub. The eastern and western river branches curve around the city like protective arms, allowing for a port at the city's southern edge where the rivers meet before continuing downstream.

Defensive structures include walls and sentries positioned along the riverbanks to prevent piracy, ensuring safe passage for the numerous ships that navigate the converging waterways. Housing clusters densely near the banks, providing residents with easy access to the river for both transportation and daily water needs.

## Economy and Trade

Luminaris serves as a manufacturing powerhouse for the entire [[Luminaris (Province)]], specializing in:

- **Glassworks**: Fine glassware and decorative items
- **Furniture crafting**: Both utilitarian and luxury pieces
- **Metalworking**: Advanced techniques producing tools and decorative metal goods
- **Services**: Banking, legal services, and artisan guilds

As the juncture of all major trade routes in the region, the city benefits from its position upstream from importer/exporter traffic while remaining downstream of raw material producers. This gives the populace exceptional access to both finished goods and essential supplies, making them among the best-supplied citizens in Luminara.

## History

Originally a major city in the Vaeloran Coalition, Luminaris was burned down during the [[First Wayfire]]. After the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]] and the subsequent reclamation, it became the capital site, though it had to be rebuilt from the ashes of its former self.
