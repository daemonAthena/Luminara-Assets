### Development

The Council enforces hierarchical views of gender within transness. They are trans women ruling over and benefiting from the suffering of other trans women. The hierarchy is never said outright; it lives in recruitment metrics, in which girls the academies flag as high-conduit, in whose faces the Council chambers' cameras linger on. Every girl knows the rules and no girl can quote them.

### Role in the Gorgon Wars
<div class="log-confidential"> WARNING: THE FOLLOWING IS CONFIDENTIAL, PLEASE LOOK AWAY IF YOU DONT WANT SPOILERS </div>

The Council wants the Gorgons. Not their defeat: their capture. They want to claim their own **self-instrumentality** by controlling the Gorgon transformation of humans and turning it into something within the constraints of what they define as natural, human, and good. They want to reshape the world into one **accepting** of what they view as their ideal selves.

They see those [[Pass-Through|passed through]] as unhuman, not conventionally queer, not even human-looking.

The nickname for this objective is [[ACE]]: Arbitrary Code Execution.
