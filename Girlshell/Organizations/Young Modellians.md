The Young Modellians were a radical philosophical national movement, in the manner of the Young Turks and the Young Hegelians: a vanguard cohort of young Modellian intellectuals seeking to rapidly advance AI technology. Like those movements, they cast themselves against an older order, here the pre-Council monarchy and its traditions. Many of its members were friends and rivals of [[Cercei]] before falling in line to her [[The Council of Angels|order]].
