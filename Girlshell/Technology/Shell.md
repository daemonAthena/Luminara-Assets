## Appearance
Shells give the appearance of giant humans. They have flesh that is joined with metal plating where mechanisms have been added and require protection. Glass surrounds the womb area leading all the way up to the chest in a pattern mimicking ovaries with one crystal viewport in the forehead.

They are highly feminine and garbed in flashy sexualized clothing combined with religious imagery. Many of them have scars and features of trauma or abuse prominent on their bodies.

In the kingdom times before the rebellion, cis men took trans women and forced them to adopt the identity of males in religious conditioning camps known as convents. The Exconventica, many of whom later became [[Young Modellians]] reclaimed the nun's habit as an act of defiance. Thus it was integrated into the design of the first Shell.

## Public Standing

Shells have immersed themselves in Luminaran popular culture. Media of all varieties has been made glamourizing the lifestyle of a pilot and glorifying the beauty of the Shell they wear.

### Requirements
Pilots are the best of the best at the network. A trained girl piloting a Shell could kill you by hacking you with very limited access. Pilots are forbidden persistent connections with anyone, including each other.
