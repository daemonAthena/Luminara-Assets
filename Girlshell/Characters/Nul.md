Nul was a [[Gorgon Operations Koros]] employee and the first shell pilot.

## Biography

### Birth & Childhood
(unsupplied)

### Education
Studied under [[Sakuya the Artificer]], same cohort as [[Metiz Sanchez]] and [[Nadia Capet]].

### GOK Service
Working on a station in outer space, Nul was sent to investigate a seam in space. What she found across the seam transformed her into the first harpy. [[Cercei]] chartered [[Gorgon Operations Koros]] in response that same year. Nul had to be killed.

### Post-Story Life
Deceased.

## Relationships

### Ampersat
Her daughter. Ampersat has mixed feelings about killing Gorgons: avenging her mother's death, and watching her mother die again.

### Metiz Sanchez
Peer under Sakuya. Metiz later adopted Ampersat, which makes their relationship awkward in hindsight.

### Nadia Capet
Peer under Sakuya. Nadia killed Nul as a harpy. Nadia feels guilty and copes by doubling down on her militarism. Ampersat knows and despises her for taking the high ground; Nadia coldly ignores her.

## Trivia
- Her death was televised. She was framed as a tragic hero who ended a fallen comrade; the footage was used to demonize Gorgons and enemies of the state.
- First harpy.
