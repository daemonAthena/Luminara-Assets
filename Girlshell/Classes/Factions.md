# Conservative

Generally conservatives in Luminara are pro technocracy, rule by an educated elite versed in [[IPv7]] derived technology. They generally believe this right to rule comes from the might needed to wield this power mentally being an indicator of competence in administration.

Many of the conservative factions are derived from polycule [[cadres]] of technocrats who hold different values and function in government
