## Circe

*Circe*. Madeline Miller, 2018. 

- Witchcraft is tough manual work that allows one to eclipse gods. 
- Parental neglect and noramlized abuse created the witch Circe.
- Circe was alienated by others due to her mixed race status.
- Fucked hermes for information, casual stuff
- Due to Circe's tough upbringing and exile she was able to do more manual labor better than all the other gods, even those gifted in witchcraft.
- witnessed a lot of divine politics. Centered around luxury and arbitrary plays with mortals. Largely interest was governing. Care about getting prayed to.
- witnessed the rise of kingdoms creating legends and monsters.
- destiny and fatalism
- "She was not ruled by apetites, she ruled with them instead." - on her sister
- Wishes she could cure monsters, regrets the one she created. Human in heart unlike the gods.
- She wanted to warn areadne about the grief she'd face with maturity but opted to let her keep her childhood innocence. Presumably because its something she wished she couldve held onto.
- She becomes clever to the politics of both men and gods.
- She empathizes with beings the gods have scorned.
- Feels maternal urges
- Directly confronts her sister in the middle of the night
- Pasiphae says it is not enough to do anything good or honorable, power is all they listen to. She feels like theres a connection between her and Cerci. "Nymphs work only through the power of others." Shes a my little pony villain, "there are no friends." Cerci doesnt want to be like her.
- She lived a solitary life and romanticized prior lovers.
