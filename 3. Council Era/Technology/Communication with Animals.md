Thanks to [[IPv7]] we can communicate with **animals** directly through **drivers**.
it was discovered that not only are animals **conscious**, many have evolved enough to have fully fledged **reasoning** ability but we had never known due to their inability to **communicate**.

**Lions** in particular, were aware for hundreds of years the scale to which human society strictly **dominated** them and treated us like a **godlike** force beyond their **comprehension**.

Experimentation has been done with the **animal mind**.
