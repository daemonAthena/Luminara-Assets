**The Palladium** is the central **router** of [[IPv7]] in **Luminara**. It resides in the **palace** of the [[Council of Angels]]. It serves as an **Entry Point** to the entire **Network**. It is the center of **human connection** across the **web**.

## History
### Version 1
The Palladium was initially designed by [[Sakuya the Artificer]] to act as a super computer and central router for [[Luminet]]. It was installed with the expectation that it would serve as a final resource distribution algorithm for society eliminating the need for flawed human reasoning in important decision making.

## Construction


## Design

The Palladium is created with reinforced metal that is nigh indestructible except by a highly concentrated lazer cutter. It was designed to be immovable by any human.

## Political Function

For the Council, the Palladium is both infrastructure and symbol: the thing that cannot be negotiated with, cannot be voted out, cannot be walked away from. It is the physical proof that the network is permanent.

## Master of the Palladium

[[Circei]], head of the council, resides as **guardian** of **the Palladium**. 

She is entrusted as the **keeper** of our people's **thoughts**, **feelings**, and **memories**. When the **Gorgon Invasion** began she, ~like [[Zenia|princesses]] of old~, called for all of **Luminara** to come together and **fight** for our **lives**.
