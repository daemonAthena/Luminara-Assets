## Biography
### Birth & Childhood
Nadia was born on Dth of MM, 621 E3 in District-E. From a young age she decided she would do anything to not live that life anymore, in turn she formed a selfish streak that gained her an ominous reputation. District E has a reputation for being filled with the unskilled of Luminara due to its resistance to AI. It has historically been neglected by the council. Life is brutal for those living there. 

### Education
ROTC at university of excellence, studied under Sakuya a few grades below [[Metiz Sanchez]]

### As General under the COA
[[Council of Angels]] are her only direct reports at this stage.

At some point she adopted [[Belial]] as a mentee. She saw them both as leading similar early lives and saw herself as best equiped to help her through the life she chose.

### First Arrival at Nuei Attica
The Council of Angels to deploy Nadia and Belial to ensure that Gorgon Operations run smoothly for [[Gorgon Operations Koros|GOK]] from now on. 

When she joined the team she lead by example, disciplined soldiers getting the job done. Standards such as dress code and etiquette were enforced. Consequences for not following the rules are not fun, it's physical training or unpaid labor.  

She punished [[Artemis]] for not falling in line with the standards immediately reinforcing the message that no matter how skilled at your job you are you can't shirk even the most inane of rules. 

She became the main point of contact for GOK. 

### Luna Saga

Belial experiences a slight [[pass-through]] with the gorgon and realizes that Gorgons are living thinking beings, she wishes to tell Nadia but chooses not to out of fear of rejection. 

### Court Martial Saga
When [[Dacia]] initially appeared a fight broke out between Nadia and [[Ampersat]] over Ampersat's performance in the last sortie. The "tough love" espoused by Nadia resulted in Artemis telling Nadia off and assaulting her. This lead to Artemis' court martial but also forced Nadia to come to terms with an aspect of her leadership and parenting with Belial she had never been called out for due to her rank. 

Her court martial of Artemis comes across as an unnecessary escalation bringing her authority into question. The council is deeply displeased by her lack of accountability and leadership in unfucking the situation. She is asked to document and report the changes she is making to increase the lethality towards Gorgons of her force. Artemis is exonerated but still punished for her attack and the situation is deemed to be a failure of leadership. 

### Death
She sacrificed her life for Belial in battle with Dacia, she may have struggled with showing her love to Belial, but this was her last act of kindness to her 
 
Her biggest regret before death was not telling Belial what she knew about the Gorgons sentience because she wasn't allowed to disclose it to her.  

## Personality
She is a trauma survivor who developed a selfish and ruthless streak. She wanted nothing more than to earn her own spot on the council. Through her trauma, she became apathetic to others around her. She finally let her guard down and showed compassion for Belial, sacrificing herself in the end.
