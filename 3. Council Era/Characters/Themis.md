## Biography

### Birth & Childhood
Themis was born on 14th of Nocturn, 605 E3. She was born shortly after the exile of Zenia, she grew up in a world governed by the [[Council of Angels]].

Growing up AI was heavily integrated into society.

### Education
She attended primary school learning computation from the finest AI lecturers the government could supply. She didn't have many friends.

She went to the Circei College of Excellence in [[Modelia]] earning a master's degree in computer science with a specialization in philosophy.

### Career as an Influencer
After graduation she became a prominent influencer on [[LUMINET]] rising to the peak of the most prominent entertainment platform's algorithm.

Initially she centered her content around programming methodologies and the application of AI but eventually began to delve into talk of politics. 

She admired and promoted the Circeian systemitization of morality becoming a zealous defender and innovator in the realm of AI powered judicial decision making. Soon she replaced a judge in her region becoming the first ever vibe magistrate.

She popularized an AI harness called Sententia. Through a mixture of agents it could act as prosecutor, barrister, and judge over a case streamlining the legal process.

Her work gained the attention of [[Cercei]] and [[Ahavah]] who were looking for a likeminded and influential youth of her generation to lead the courts into a new future. At the time she seemed like she'd make a good fit. For all the temperament she lacked she could compensate with solid technology.

While the national implementation of Sententia got off to a bad start once brain box technology replaced GPU driven calculations the Sententia servers became extremely efficient. The council could standardize law with little issue across all of Luminara with just one commit.

Gaining Cercei's trust she became the head legal authority of Luminara, the Fairy.
