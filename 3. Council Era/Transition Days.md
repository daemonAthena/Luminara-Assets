# Transition Days (Luminara)

The last days of Transition represent the process of gender transition.

- **Introspection**:Artemsday

Thinking of and discovery that you are trans; a day celebrating the freshly cracked eggs or people who realize their gender again later.
- **Concealment**:Lunasday 

Denial and fears about being discovered; a day celebrating secrecy for anyone who passes or boymodes.
- **Apex**:Daciasday 

Gender euphoria and the powerful spirit that coming out initially gives you.
- **Liberation**:Zeiasday

A day celebrating boldness and freedom to dress how one desires and still feel accepted. People also go casually nude on this day.
- **Council**:Athensday

A very pious day celebrating the previous generation of trans women and their guiding hand; a day celebrating women who have reached the point where they can give back to the community with their wisdom.
- **Mourning**:Neos

A day remembering those who died to oppression. Celebrated once every 4 years on the leap year. It is seen as a time of great spiritual connection with the lost and a time of somber contemplation. Religious people used to believe it'd help prevent the world's destruction by warding off Neos. Often there are prayers that she will not return.
