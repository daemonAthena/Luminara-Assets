## Background
This is Zenia's themesong and represents her attachment to old Luminara. She deeply regrets losing it and would do anything to reclaim it.
### Stage Direction
Zenia cuts open the Council Era Luminara flag after the showy dances of the singers from the previous song and starts belting out her power ballad as everyone flees.

## Style Prompt
Synth-rock with gothic orchestral accents; clear female lead in an intimate opening, rising to urgent, forceful choruses with choir-like backing layers, Bright stately piano and warm wide strings give way to industrial percussion, sub-bass, cold arpeggiated synths, and heavy guitars; mid-tempo pulse turns stark half-time in the bridge, then surges into a final rising chorus, Detailed dramatic production, clear vocals, controlled distortion, decisive ending
