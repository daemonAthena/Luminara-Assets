Luminara C is the third movement in the recursive cycle of Luminara history. It is the wave, the synthesis.

## Temporal Position

Luminara C is a future event. The next clash is building toward it. The present era — the Council's reign, the Shell program, [[IPv7]] as infrastructure — is the thesis the wave will negate. The synthesis is not a person or a battle; it is a protocol shift that rewrites what a girl can be.

## Mechanism

The wave moves through technology: a protocol, Shell, or network shift. It emerges from the network itself rather than being engineered by either side. The Council built [[IPv7]] to control; now the protocol is about to do something they did not write.

## Relation to Previous Clashes

- A: Luminara A, the first clash, thesis.
- B: Luminara B, the antithesis.
- C: Luminara C, the wave, synthesis.
- New clash: the next cycle, a new thesis.

The wave creates the new clash. The cycles repeat.
