## History
### Founding
The organization was founded in Era 2 under the reign of [[Lucretia]].  Originally it started as a regional community organized via instant messengers and forums.

They would organize cuddle parties, picnics, meals, group get-togethers. To fight the loneliness of the time and possibly counteract the effect AI was having on people.  They thrived on intimacy.
### Development
After the [[Angelic Insurrection]] intimacy became weaponized. Many lost the ability to discern when they were being manipulated due to the introduction of new technology.

This led to the organization also weaponizing intimacy.

### Role in the Gorgon Wars
During the Gorgon Wars they started to adopt rejected pilots into their ranks quickly transforming them from a backwater cult to a nation spanning organization. 

They organized effectively govern the sex trade by the late 690's E3.
