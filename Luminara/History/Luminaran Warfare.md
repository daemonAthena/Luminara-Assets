Since the first settlers rowed up the [[Estroveil]], whom later chronicles name the [[Ethnography of Luminara|Valerans]], warfare has been present and changing, growing more and more sophisticated. In the beginning militia were mustered to guard towns and cities from raiders driven to attack by the scarcity of resources.

# Raiding
To begin with the manner a raider would execute his or her attack. Now among the trans, it was common for many people to dwell close to the center of a settlement. Among the cis it was the exact opposite, often cities and villages were laid out with a great deal of intentional spacing between buildings.

This fundamentally changed how raiders would attack each tribe. The trans were peaceful but were quick to muster in times of attack. Vagabonds would take advantage of this by spreading out, raiding the perimeter of their settlements, and quickly absconding. Adapting to this the trans were the first on the continent to build walls.

Unlike their trans counterparts cis villages were more well armed, every citizen carried a weapon often, but spread out focused on defending their own properties. Raiders would frequently use distraction and envelopment with larger numbers to win an advantage over them. The high priest of a town, seeing this, would often set out to establish town watches manned in shifts by men of the village.
# Early Luminara

Walls could hold back a basic attack but the only material for walling the early trans settlers of Luminara had in excess was wood making them vulnerable to arson. Suddenly raids became even deadlier. To defend the city center they developed a central weapons cache and trained the citizens to fight at range with javelins and bows. Walls were adapted to have stairs for defenders to pick off foes approaching. Fire brigades were formed to manage arson fetching water from the river.

As Luminara and surrounding settlments grew, land banditry subsided and piracy up and down the Estroveil which defined Luminara's borders became the norm. People from poorer towns trying their luck on a group they thought to be weaker. Soon the Luminarans created early ships.
