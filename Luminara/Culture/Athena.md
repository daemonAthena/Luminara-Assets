# Athena

Athena is the goddess of wisdom, strategy, metalworking, leadership, familial love, and adoption.

She closely echoes the Hellenic Athena, but in this telling she is no Medusa-punisher. Instead she is the patron of civilization: centralization, city-building, and strategy. She mirrors the autistic joy of strategy gaming and city-builders, and the yearning for close-knit, chosen familial support.

In [[Calanthiad|The Calanthiad]], she guides Calantha's sword and shield in warfare. She is at odds with [[Zeia]].
