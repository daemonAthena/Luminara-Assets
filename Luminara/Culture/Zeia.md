> Zeia is the storm that answers vengeance, and the tide that answers revolution.

Zeia is the goddess of storms, tides, rivers, vengeance, raw power, and revolution. Her domain is the tumultuous and unpredictable forces of nature, and she is invoked by those seeking to harness the destructive and transformative power of those elements.

[[Athena]] does not like her.
