Post Blooming War Luminara is approximately 50% transgender women with a vast array of cultures. While culture is trans dominated, biology does not dictate cultural norms, so even if someone is cis they can still be considered a member of a trans culture.

Despite this the largest contingent culture on the island is the cisgender Grainwise who make up 20% of the population and cover 15% of its surface area.

# Trans Cultures
## Exconventica
The Exconventica were trans women consigned to convents during the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]] and freed after it was completed. They mostly inhabit [[Thronaria]] AKA the old Ancien Patriarchate Crownlands.
## Aurelian
Aurelians are trans settlers of the [[Province of Aurelia]], formerly a part of [[Greatmill]] in the Ancien patriarchate. Their culture is rooted in their struggle as a community to blend into cisgender society around them during the patriarchate for survival.
## Victorian
Victorians reside in the [[Province of Elowynn]]. They are the descendants of rural trans femmes who managed to find some semblance of peace and open society in the rural forests and plains of the province known as Vichi during their diaspora. They were the bulk of the Luminaran army during the Blooming War. As such they call themselves after victory. They grew out of the [[Artemai]], the once nomadic [[Traveling Camp]] who after the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]] settled into the rural life they had always yearned for.

"Vichi" is the name cis colonizers gave the region. Those colonizers did take root there, but they failed to press into its heartland, for the waters were treacherous. They laid claim to all of it on paper, yet the Victorians already existed quietly in the valleys between [[Ancien]] and [[Bloodmarch]].
## Veteran
Veterans mostly reside in the [[Province of Rowan]]. They are descendants of the expert heavy fighters of the Blooming War who mostly hailed from the rolling hills of [[Bloodmarch]]. They are the most victimized and traumatized of the cultures due to Bloodmarch's cruel policies towards trans women so many Veterans have PTSD and specifically accomodate for it in their day-to-day functions. 

They are a clan of warriors, proud to be first to take up arms if the republic were to ever come under attack because they fought hard for their freedom.
## Fidelans
A very religious clan which survived on the sidelines of the war, watching and praying for victory. Mostly consigned to a peninsula near bloodmarch they didnt have the ability to organize and fight until the war was pretty much over already. They grew up surrounded by fundamentalists trying to take away their rights and served as a refuge to any Veteran who found herself south of the Bloodmarch hills. They mostly reside in [[Blessara]].
## Artemai
Also called the [[Traveling Camp]], the Artemai are the people who diverged from the [[Valeran]] stock of the north, the same communities among whom Calantha dwelt during the [[First Wayfire]]. Forced to adapt, they became a different people altogether. Where the settled Valerans kept their walled towns, the Artemai turned survivalist and nomadic, wandering and keeping to themselves. They were secretive and unafraid to raid for food when scarcity pressed. They held their people as family, practiced polyamory openly, and reared children as a communal effort rather than within isolated households.

Their name and lineage trade back to [[Artemis]]. Through the trans adoption of Calantha, and her symbolic adoption of everyone in that community, the Artemai count themselves among Artemis's line.

The Artemai came into themselves in the long stretch between the [[First Wayfire]] and the start of the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]], for it is in that interval that they trace their descent from Calantha. Though they wandered as survivalist nomads, they had always yearned for a settled life. After the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]] made settlement possible, they took to it as settled rural folk, keeping the pastoral ways they were used to. In settling they became the [[Victorians]], the victorious ones.

Calantha was the mother of the Artemai and lived among them. She led the people in captivity.

## Dolls
Cosmopolitan settlers from every walk of life joining together to complete the new project of a unified Luminara. They make up the population of the capital. They are truly a melting pot of trans culture: highly urbanized, strong rural identity, exilic memory, fierce loyalty, avante guarde, and highly cultured. They like to drape themselves in the aesthetic of nobility but it is a taboo to act as another trans woman's better.
