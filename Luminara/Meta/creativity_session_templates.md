# Creativity Session — Question Templates

Purpose: during a Luminara creativity session, every question Hermes asks is
captured here as a *simplified, reusable template* (a prompt skeleton Cecìl can
reuse in future sessions, or feed as a grounding question when scribing). One
document, append-only, newest at bottom.

Convention per entry:
- **Q#** — the live question as asked
- **Template** — the stripped-down reusable version (checkboxes / placeholders)
- **Used for** — what it surfaces

---

## Q1 — Direction of attention
**Asked:** where does your attention want to go right now?
**Template:**
> **Direction check** — what do you want to explore right now?
> - [ ] Deepen an *existing* thread (e.g. Valerans/Calantha, convent collapse, Blooming War aftermath, House Piast, the Artemai)
> - [ ] Open a *new* corner (a place, people, or practice not yet scribed)
> - [ ] Explore a *theme / contradiction / what-if* (structural, not a location)
**Used for:** picking the scope before generating detail.

## Q2 — Emotional / thematic register
**Asked:** what's the emotional or thematic charge you're in the mood for?
**Template:**
> **Register check** — what emotional tone are you in the mood for?
> - [ ] Warm / tender (chosen family, trans-femme reimaginings)
> - [ ] Bitter / sharp (tyranny, exile, collapse)
> - [ ] Unresolved / tense (a contradiction left hanging)
> - [ ] Surprise me (Hermes throws a tension at you)
**Used for:** setting the affective register of the scene/lore.

## Q3 — Generation scale (inside-out vs outside-in)
**Asked:** what's the smallest unit you want to generate first?
**Template:**
> **Generation scale** — where do you want to start?
> - [ ] Outside-in — large structure/event first, then populate
> - [ ] Inside-out — one small concrete thing first, then let it spread
> - [ ] Follow the energy
**Used for:** picking the scale before generating detail.

## Q4 — A Valeran everyday practice
**Asked:** give one ordinary thing a Valeran does that a Wayfire / Traveling Camp
dweller would NOT do.
**Template:**
> **Cultural anchor** — name one practice that defines this group by contrast.
> - [ ] Domestic / household practice
> - [ ] Social / community ritual
> - [ ] Craft / subsistence habit
> - [ ] Taboo or avoidance
**Used for:** grounding a culture in a concrete, repeatable act (not a survey).

## Q5 — Interdependence vs individualism (structure of care)
**Asked:** the Valeran priestesses are war-refugee healers; their culture is
interdependent (contrast: Artemai steppe-individualist survivalism). How does
that interdependence show up structurally?
**Template:**
> **Care structure** — how is mutual aid organized in this culture?
> - [ ] Communal resource (granaries, shared labor, common store)
> - [ ] Kinship / chosen-family web (who is "yours" beyond blood)
> - [ ] A specialist caste that serves all (e.g. refugee-healer priesthood)
> - [ ] Ritual obligation (debt, reciprocity, gift economy)
**Used for:** turning a value ("we're interdependent") into institutions.
