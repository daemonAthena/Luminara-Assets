The LUMINARA calendar has 12 months of 30 days followed by TRANSITION, 5 days in a common year and 6 in a leap year. A year is 365 days, or 525600 minutes. Year numbers do not reset at era boundaries.

The source export is `Meta/Luminara Timeline.json`. It stores dates in minutes since founding. Year values below are minutes divided by 525600, rounded to two decimals. An empty End means the export records no end value.
