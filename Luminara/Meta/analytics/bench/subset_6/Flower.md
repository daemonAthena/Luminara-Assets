> A symbol of love and delicate value, the Flower (symbol ƒ, code FLR) is the cherished currency of [[Luminara]]. Each paper note is a beautiful and delicate work of art, featuring cute flowers and stylized portraits of the founders, drawn in a moe (cute anime) style.

The name "Flower" holds deep significance in [[Luminara]]. The term is a term of endearment for a trans girl, signifying her as a blossoming and valuable individual who is delicate and should be treated kindly. This cultural significance inspired the choice of floral themes for the currency, as it evokes feelings of love and safety. The designs are carefully crafted to reflect this sentiment, and no single flower dominates the currency. Instead, each note is unique, with a real flower pressed into it from a wild valley in the [[Province of Aurelia]], where all the different flower types on the island grow.

Behind the design and printing of these exquisite notes is the expert [[Splorin Florin]], who not only designs the bills but also selects her successor through an apprenticeship process. Her duties are considered sacred, and she ensures that the currency remains a symbol of love and care.

The actual printing of the flowers is a more advanced process handled by the technocratic folks in [[Vaelora]]. Despite this, the final product remains a handcrafted piece of art, reflecting the values and culture of [[Luminara]].

The charm of the Flower currency lies not only in its design but also in the care and craftsmanship that goes into each note. It is a testament to the deep-rooted cultural values of [[Luminara]], where every transaction is a reminder of love, respect, and the delicate balance of life.
