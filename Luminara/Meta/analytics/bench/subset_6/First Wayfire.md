# First Wayfire

The First Wayfire was the colonial war waged by [[John Wayfire]], a cis patriarch of the [[Ancien]] empire, against the trans-led peoples of the island. The conflict takes its name from him; "Wayfire" is an eponym earned through atrocity, akin to being called Adolf von Holocaust.

It drove the [[Valeran]] people to flee the [[Vaeloran Coalition]] and exiled Provincial Luminarans northward. The [[Artemai]] trace the beginning of their people to the long stretch between the First Wayfire and the [[Blooming War]]. [[Calantha]] dwelt among the communities that became the Artemai during this time.
