Luminara, officially the Kingdom of Luminara, is a sovereign island nation located in the western seas. It is a constitutional monarchy and the world's first modern nation-state explicitly founded by and for trans women. Established in the aftermath of the Blooming War, Luminara serves as a global beacon of trans self-determination, sovereignty, and cultural flourishing.

The country is renowned for its distinctive tricolor flag (pastel pink, white, and light blue), its central national mythos of the "Blooming", a lifelong process of authentic becoming, and its unique blend of Enlightenment-inspired republican values with openly affectionate, polyamorous court and parliamentary culture. Luminara operates as an anti-capitalist constitutional monarchy, with political power shared between the reigning Princess (currently the line descending from Seraphina the Liberator) and a parliament organized around six major factions: the Rurals, Urbans, Soldiers, Merchants, Technologists, and the Faithful.

Spanning diverse landscapes from the misty northern valleys to fertile southern heartlands and coastal archipelagos, Luminara is home to approximately 28 million citizens, the vast majority of whom are trans women. Its capital and largest city is Luminara City, located in the Province of Luminaris. The nation maintains a policy of radical hospitality toward trans exiles and refugees while fiercely guarding its hard-won independence from external powers.

Luminara is widely regarded as one of the most significant social experiments of the modern era, representing both a dramatic break from the patriarchal Ancien Patriarchate that once ruled the island and a living embodiment of trans resilience, creativity, and statecraft.

Prior to the organization of the great trans state, a polity consisting mostly of cis people named [[Ancien Patriarchate|Ancien]] ruled the island of Luminara. During this time colonies were established by both trans and cis people to escape the collapsing government of the United States. Trans people were driven to settle by a global estrogen shortage and the oppression which followed so their numbers were larger. The cis settlers were driven by a fondness and nostalgia for their old way of life prior to the fall of the USA. 

As the two colonies developed cis and trans culture on the island developed differently. Trans femmes were largely seen as predatory by the cisgender colonists, fearing they were going to rape or convert them reducing their number and fertility. By their measure a culture died if it was outbred. There is no evidence (obviously) that the trans colonists ever tried to do this. Some cisgender women voluntarily joined and became partners with trans femme colonists but this was always a consensual arrangement.

Eventually patriarchal norms developed amongst the ancien and became their society's most defining trait. Women became the property of their husbands and had their movement and career choices limited. To the Luminarans this seemed like a societal regression. Many female refugees ended up fleeing to the Luminaran colonies thus boosting their population further. It was exceedingly rare for a trans femme to produce offspring that was not also trans femme despite the rate of transition being generally low. This is when people began to speculate that transgender trait reproduction had a biological component.

Eventually though, the ancien agenda of "outbreeding" the opposition came to fruition and their colonies overtook the Luminaran colony. In their first war the colony was destroyed and trans women of the colony were either scattered or killed. Moving forward a new regime would develop in ancien called The Patriachate.

Trans femme society continued to develop under the oppressive Ancien regime. Trans communities moved from the once bustling city of luminaris to various fields and villages across the continent to hide from the persecutive and watchful eyes of the patriarchate most active in their city strongholds. Most trans women living in the cities of this time were stealth to avoid detection by the governing religious authorities and feudal lords.

Villages served as an escape which allowed trans femme culture to develop. Trans femme villagers adopted new models of family structure, identity, education, economy, and relationships which would serve as the backbone of Luminaran culture. Adoption became the norm for tracing genology since often trans women were exiled from their families for being their authentic selves. Funerary rites and record keeping ran separate from ancien bureaucracy keeping chronicles to preserve identity when cis society refused to respect a woman's chosen name. Village assemblies created low budget curriculum which became a self directed learning initiative promoting self education. Polyamory became a norm for trans women since patriarchal oppression resulted in epidemic loneliness but also a rejection of values such as monogamy. 

As government raids became prominent against these communities policies were instituted which banned cis men and women from being inducted into these societies for safety.

The founding Myth of Luminara is the Blooming War. In the old times patriarchs ruled the land. The government of the island had long eroded into a stagnant theocracy which sought to enforce draconian gender roles, strict hierarchy, extreme stoicism, and normalize exploitation of the people. I dont know what the nation was called at this time but the government is known to the Luminarans as the Ancien Patriarchate.

The war began with the arrest of innocents. Trans women were deemed enemies of the state, rounded up and sent to convents for forced conversion. But among them there was a bold woman who was known to the community as a friend and fierce advocate, The [[Seraphina|Princess Seraphina]]. Devestated by news of the arrest of her loved ones, she immediately rode about on horseback going city to city and sounding a trumpet flanked by cohorts of her swelling army. The occupants in each of the cities could do nothing to stop her from recruiting across the rustic countryside. 

Women of all kinds fled there and found refuge in cottages and barns. They became a self sufficient guerilla force. Once they were organized they defeated the army of the state and besieged the capital. Her two most trusted lieutenants and secretaries were Aurelia the golden haired of the city and Elowynn the brunette of the countryside. Elowynn was a rustic hunter in the mold of [[Artemis]] who valued the rural culture her people preserved; the province of [[Province of Elowynn|Elowynn (Province)]] is named for her, as she was from that area. Aurelia, golden haired and from the trade city of [[Eos]], defected to the rebellion and coordinated its logistics, navy, and affairs of state with a Hamiltonian energy. All together they broke down the walls of the castle and freed the women imprisoned in the convents. The grey lord abdicated to the princess at sword point and the war was over, a new era began.

## Reconstruction

A traumatized boy who lived through the siege and the war named Cecil, the Hermit Prince became a confidant of the princess in the coming years as the new government of Luminara was framed. A constitutional monarchy run by trans women, for trans women. The princess oversaw the new parliament consisting of elected representatives from across the land. New factions arose to represent the classes that encapsulated the new anticaptialist Luminaran society

- Rurals
- Urbans
- Soldiers
- Merchants
- Technologists
- Faithful

During the reconstruction era the hermit prince would right letters to the princess making her aware of troubles faced by every day folks while lively discussions were held in the capital city. In the end trans women had a place they could call home. No longer would they be exiles.

# Politics
Luminara is a devolved constitutional monarchy with anarchist characteristics established after the fall of the ancien patriachate. Their governing traditions are deeply rooted in the transgender experience and queer identity.

All of Luminara is divided into 10 administrative regions, each with its own unique history and culture.
- [[Luminaris (Province)|Luminaris]], the capital province
- Modellia, the seat of tech savvy bureaucrats
- Seeded Province, the breadbasket of the nation
- Aurelia, the trade capital of the nation
- Crownveil, a great expanse and the site of ruins from the blooming war
- Elowynn, home to the cottage culture which sprung up during the capture of innocents
- Thronaria, an eastern capital of sorts. Hellenism is very popular there
- Blessaria, site of great temple complexes
- [[Rowan]], a valley frontier where soldiers go to retire in safety
