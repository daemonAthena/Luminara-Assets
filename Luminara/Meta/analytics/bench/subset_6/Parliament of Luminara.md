> The heart of Luminara's governance lies in the Parliament of Luminara, a unique legislative body where the will of the people is the law.

The Parliament of Luminara is the legislature of Luminara, elected through local assemblywomen, each representing a borough or community. These assemblywomen, known as Angels, are sworn to convey the exact will of their people, ensuring that their voices are heard. When not acting as messengers, they are referred to as Daemons, engaging in the legislative process.

Each Angel listens intently to the proceedings of Parliament and carries the unanimous will of her community back to [[Luminaris (Province)|Luminaris]], the capital city. This process ensures that the laws passed by Parliament truly reflect the desires of the populace. The Princess of Luminara acts as the leader, guiding the Angels towards agreement but exerting no control over them. Her role is to provide wisdom and support, serving as a beloved icon for the nation.

Parliament convenes in [[Luminaris (Province)|Luminaris]], where all Angels gather to deliberate on proposed legislation. Once consensus is reached, the Princess formalizes the law, ensuring that it becomes official. The government of Luminara is a Devolved Parliamentary Constitutional Monarchy with Anarcho-communist features, represented by six key factions: the Rurals, Urbans, Soldiers, Merchants, Technologists, and the Faithful.

The unique structure of the Parliament of Luminara ensures that the will of the people is always at the forefront of legislative decisions, creating a balanced and representative form of governance.
