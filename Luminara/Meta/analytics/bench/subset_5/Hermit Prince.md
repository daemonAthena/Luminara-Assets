The [[Hermit Prince]] is a femboy reporter who travels through [[Luminara]], quietly documenting the state of the people and society. His role is to report back to the princess, advocating for change and assistance. He is also a philosopher who lived through the [[Blooming War]] as a child. The [[Hermit Prince]] believed in the necessity of a watcher to keep society's powers in check, viewing himself as a gadfly much like Socrates. He is actually bisexual and is prone to fluster because he's also young.
