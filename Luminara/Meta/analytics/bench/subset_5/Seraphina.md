> The Princess who sounded the trumpet, and bore every doll's sorrow as her own.

Seraphina, called the Liberator, is the Princess of [[Luminara]] and the figure who began the [[Blooming War]] by riding out and sounding her trumpet city to city.

## The Meaning of "Princess"
Among trans femmes the word carries a meaning distinct from the old monarchies. "Queen" is generally reserved for drag queens, carrying that association, but "princess" among trans femmes means a younger, graceful, attractive, and benevolent person whom one loves. This cultural sense shaped how the Luminaran people came to view the monarchy itself.

## Role as Monarch
Her role as monarch of Luminara is mostly ceremonial, yet she is active in her duty. She keeps the peace and guides the competing political factions beneath the singular banner of trans feminine sisterhood.

## The Weight She Carries
She bears a weight on her heart. She worries for each doll as though they were her child, and has taken up her position as a symbol despite the toll it takes. Her partners and her people love her for precisely this.

## Lineage and Adoption
Among Luminarans, lineage is traced generally through adoption rather than blood, established through bonds between two people, often a woman farther along in her transition and a newly blooming woman. This is how the lines of succession and belonging are formed.

## The Word "Doll"
In trans femme speech, a doll is an affectionate way of referring to another trans woman. The Princess reinforced this usage heavily in Luminara, and it took root so deeply that the creole culture of trans femmes residing in the capital from all over the island, renowned for their beautiful modes of dress, came to be called dolls.
