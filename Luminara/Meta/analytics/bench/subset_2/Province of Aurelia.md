# Province of Aurelia

The Province of Aurelia is the southern province containing the city of [[Eos]]. It was named in honor of [[Aurelia]], the golden-haired lieutenant of [[Seraphina]] who hailed from Eos. Formerly part of [[Greatmill]] in the Ancien Patriarchate, its culture is rooted in the struggle of trans settlers to blend into cisgender society around them for survival.
