Maris of the Tideward is a revered figure among the exiled trans communities of the coast, known for her exceptional navigational skills and unwavering courage. Born in the bustling port city of Seaforge, Maris was a skilled cartographer and navigator, charting the treacherous waters surrounding the coastal cities with meticulous precision. Her journey to becoming a folk hero began when the Ancien Patriarchate's forces launched a brutal crackdown on trans communities, forcing many to flee to the open sea.

Maris played a crucial role in the exodus, guiding ships through the perilous waters to safety. Her knowledge of the tides, currents, and hidden reefs was unmatched, and her ability to navigate through storms and calms alike saved countless lives. She became a symbol of hope and resilience, her stories of bravery and ingenuity passed down through generations. Today, Maris is often invoked in times of crisis, her legend inspiring those who seek to chart their own paths, both literally and figuratively, in the wake of the revolution.
