> The heart of the Calanthiad beats in the story of Calantha, the coolest and most badass hero of Luminara.

Calantha was a city planner for [[Luminaris (Province)|Luminaris]], overseeing the urban development of the city which had been in development for over two centuries. Her work included zoning services, planning the development of river systems, building bridges, and implementing evacuation measures. She was dedicated to her role, and her passion for strategy and leadership was evident in her meticulous planning. Calantha's ultimate wish was to die defending the palaces she had built.

From a young age, Calantha was mentored by [[Athena]], the goddess of wisdom and war. Athena guided her as a soldier, teaching her to protect the land she called home. Calantha's ambition to lead was fueled by Athena's mentorship, and she became a dedicated fighter. However, during the outset of the [[First Wayfire]], her hopes were dashed. She lost many battles and, symbolically, her sense of hope when [[Athena]] left her. Devastated, Calantha left her family and fled to the north, where she stayed among [[Vaeloran Coalition|Vaeloran]] refugees in Valeria.

In Valeria, Calantha chased hollow lives, ignoring the state of her country. Her survivors' guilt and PTSD eventually drove her to the forest. It was there that she nearly starved. One day, she was found by [[Artemis]], the huntress goddess, who shared food with her and refused to let her live alone. Over four years, Calantha trained with Artemis, learning to hunt and sharing her regrets about [[Vaelora]]. After these years, Artemis gave Calantha a carved wooden box containing the bow of light, offering her a choice: to survive, teach, or live alone. Calantha chose to avenge her fallen family and save the rest.

This is the heart of [[Calanthiad|The Calanthiad]]: Calantha rode home by horse, a one-woman army, shooting her way through castles down the river to [[Vaelora]], using the laser bow to free every trans girl locked within. She took their food, wreaked havoc, and wiped out the Wayfire bureaucracy that had harmed her family. The survivors returned to the forest oases where [[Artemis]] lived, and they built their first camps, practicing migrational farming. They became the [[Artemai]], and Calantha is their mother.
