The Saltwind Convoy is a merchant fleet of trans women refugees that has become a vital lifeline for the coastal cities of Luminara. Established in the aftermath of the revolution against the Ancien Patriarchate, the convoy's primary purpose is to facilitate the trade and exchange of goods between the various cities while providing a safe haven and a means of transportation for those displaced by the conflict.

Each ship in the convoy is captained by a seasoned navigator, often a trans woman with a deep understanding of the local waters and winds. The fleet includes a mix of cargo ships, fishing vessels, and small transport boats, all painted in a distinctive palette of vibrant blues and silvers to symbolize the waves and the sky. The convoy's route is carefully planned to avoid the more dangerous stretches of sea, ensuring the safety of its passengers and cargo.

The Saltwind Convoy plays a crucial role in maintaining the economic stability of the coastal cities. It transports goods such as fish, textiles, and herbs from city to city, ensuring that resources are distributed efficiently. More importantly, it serves as a symbol of resilience and unity, bringing together communities that share a common history and a collective struggle.
