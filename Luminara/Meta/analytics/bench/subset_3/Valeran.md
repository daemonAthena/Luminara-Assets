# Valeran

The Valerans were a people who fled the [[Vaeloran Coalition]] before the [[First Wayfire]]. Exiled Provincial Luminarans from the [[First Wayfire]], they are the direct descendants of the original settlers of Luminara. They colonized the rural north of what was then known as the [[Duchy of Wayfire]] and brought with them their seafaring culture.

After the brutal destruction of their cities they were the first to adopt the strategy of fleeing to the countryside but still kept their sedentary walled communities rather than adopting the [[Traveling Camp]]. They were separated from the Wayfires by a plateau offering geographic barriers to the encroaching urbanized south.

They were integral in the blockading efforts during the [[Blooming War]] and remained a separate and distinct culture even when their ancestral homelands were recolonized by a variety of trans people.
