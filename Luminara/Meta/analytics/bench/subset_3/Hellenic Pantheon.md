## ??
A god of chaos whom no one can understand unless they are blessed by him. They become mutually intelligeble at the cost of their perception of reality and ability to communicate.

## Artemis
Goddess of hunt, woodcarving, defense, femininity, polyamory, and carnal love

## Athena
Goddess of wisdom, strategy, metalworking, leadership, familial love, governance, and adoption

## Zeia
Goddess of storms, tides, rivers, vengeance, raw power, and revolution. Athena doesn't like her.

## Luna
Goddess of divination, magic, mysteries, shade, the moon, and rest. She guides the dead to find peace. Contradictory figure since she both conceals and reveals information.

## Dacia
Goddess of wilderness, beasts, feral mindedness, bloodshed, berserkers, and raw sexual passion. She's not on good terms with Artemis who keeps killing her spawn for sport nor Athena who hates how unpredictable she is.

## Neos 
goddess of life and death, harvest, the sun, butterflies (matamorphosis), growth. Mother of the gods. She was overthrown and cast down into the earth where she dictates the cycle of life. The gods opposed her tyranny and fought to free themselves from it hence how they became immortal.
