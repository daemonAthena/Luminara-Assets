# Artemai

Also called the [[Traveling Camp]], the Artemai are the people who diverged from the [[Valeran]] stock of the north, the same communities among whom [[Calantha]] dwelt during the [[First Wayfire]]. Forced to adapt, they became a different people altogether. Where the settled Valerans kept their walled towns, the Artemai turned survivalist and nomadic, wandering and keeping to themselves. They were secretive and unafraid to raid for food when scarcity pressed. They held their people as family, practiced polyamory openly, and reared children as a communal effort rather than within isolated households.

Their name and lineage trade back to [[Artemis]]. Through the trans adoption of Calantha, and her symbolic adoption of everyone in that community, the Artemai count themselves among Artemis's line.

The Artemai came into themselves in the long stretch between the [[First Wayfire]] and the start of the [[Blooming War]], for it is in that interval that they trace their descent from Calantha. Though they wandered as survivalist nomads, they had always yearned for a settled life. After the [[Blooming War]] made settlement possible, they took to it as settled rural folk, keeping the pastoral ways they were used to. In settling they became the [[Victorians]], the victorious ones.

Calantha was the mother of the Artemai and lived among them. She led the people in captivity.
