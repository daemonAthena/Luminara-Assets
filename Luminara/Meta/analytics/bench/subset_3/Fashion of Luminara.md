> As varied as the dolls who wear it.

Luminaran dress has no single uniform. Like irl subcultures, it spans a wide range of styles worn by individual dolls.

## Styles
- **Goth** aesthetics, alongside the various branches of **Lolita** fashion.
- The dykey "just came back from the steel mill" look, practical and rugged.
- A more **American colonial** style, marked by ruffled neckties and buttoned blue jackets.

These sit alongside the national tricolor (pastel pink, white, and light blue) carried elsewhere in Luminaran symbolism, but everyday fashion is left to the wearer.
