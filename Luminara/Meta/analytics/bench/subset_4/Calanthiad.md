She is a brave woman, she would fight till her end for her fellow sister. She would give the clothes off her back to save her friends. She tragically loses them anyways. She is stubborn in her goal of reclaiming her homeland and then finding a new homeland. She is in denial of the impending fate of her people. This mistake leads to much death and suffering for her. The people around her are defeatist and have accepted it. She learns to acknowledge the new situation shes in and hold onto her hope fighting for a better tomorrow regardless and leads by example to create a new home.

Another struggle, she always feels like she's fighting for her life.

The question she dances around is "Is it better to keep your head down and survive or stand up and fight?" She answer she rests on is "it's best to pick your battles but never resign yourself to defeat."

# Summary

Her saga lasts 20 years

For 5 years she holds out against the Ancien soldiers in the Wayfire.

She doesn't want to abandon her country so she resided in exile in her own home for 5 more years trying to raise a host to rebel and reclaim her home against the advice of the gods. She fails and loses her loved ones (much like Calantha when she gets fucked up by the cyclops)

For 5 years she lives amongst the refugees in the northern nation of New Vaelora but is consumed by survivors guilt and abandons her partner, fleeing into the woods.

For 5 years she lives in the woods training with Artemis to rescue her family and clan from the Ancien province of Wayfire.

Then her story finished with a successful exodus and several miracles.


# Act 1
## Call To Flee Her Homeland & Desire to Defend her Home
She sees hundreds of trans women fleeing north to a new Vaeleria. She scoffs and calls them cowards. She arms herself for battle pledging her loyalty to the princess who calls for the participation of all trans women.
## Divine Guidance and The Wayfire
Athena guides her sword and shield in warfare and she is unstoppable in battle but it is nothing to the hordes of savages wielding scavenged firearms. It takes an army to win. Athena advises her to retreat which she refuses for a desire to help her fellow women so Athena leaves her to her own devices. The wayfire happens slaughtering anyone left to stand against the empire. 
## Hiding Amongst the Enemy
Among the enemy she hides plotting alone to avenge the princess and reclaim her home. She raises and trains a new army and drills them to fight. She could only find a few motivated women to fight with her.
## The Slaughter & Exile
They are handedly defeated in battle since they are outnumbered. Everyone she ever knew is slaughtered and only she survives in exile.
# Act 2
## Vaelerian Love & Survivors Guilt
She flees to Vaeleria to live amongst those she previously called cowards ashamed and humiliated. She falls in love trying to ignore the new order to their south like everyone else who is calmly accepting that their demise. She can't help but remember the lives lost and she wants to come back she wants to fight again. She leaves behind everything willingly this time and goes deep in the woods to find shelter.
## Apostasis in the Deep Woods
In the woods she is trained in combat and self sustenance by a mysterious nymph who gives her reflection on her goals. She realizes it's her role not to reclaim Luminara but to carry its spirit wherever she goes. She recieves a bow blessed by Artemis in a sacred Grove by the river and is commanded to go back to lead her people to a new home.
## The Invasion
She fights to rescue not to reclaim instead recognizing reclamation can only happen once we are allowed to flourish. She kills hundreds and saves thousands.

## The Reckoning of House Wayfire
For the war [[John Wayfire]] had waged, Calantha took her revenge. The bow [[Artemis]] gave her, blessed in the sacred grove by the river, could loose beams of light, and with it she struck down any who stood against her. Moving through the occupied [[Wayfire (Province)]], she rescued the trans femmes still held there and cut down every adult of [[House Wayfire]] she found on the way out, sparing only the children.

## The Goddesses' Design

The goddesses intended for the heroine to learn through trials and tribulations that her true role was to carry [[Luminara]]'s spirit rather than reclaim it. [[Athena]] vaguely gestures at this revelation when she tells the heroine to stop fighting after the [[First Wayfire]] ends.
