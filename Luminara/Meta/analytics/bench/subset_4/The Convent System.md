> In the shadow of [[Thronaria]], a network of religious reeducation houses emerged, a system of conversion camps and coerced labor aimed at redefining 'manhood' among the trans women sent there.

The Convent System was a brutal and systematic program established in the [[Ancien]] province of [[Thronaria]], where the capital itself became the epicenter of this oppressive practice. The system was designed to enforce Messianic Christianity, a fundamentalist version of evangelicalism that placed the Ancien patriarch as the ultimate hand of Christ, his reincarnated self on earth. This doctrine was imposed with an iron fist, leaving no room for dissent or individuality.

Initially, the Convent System targeted hidden trans women in [[Thronaria]], where information on the practice was limited. The capital province, with its rigid bureaucracy, enforced these practices with meticulous thoroughness. However, as the system spread outward, local lords in other provinces took a more lax approach, often too preoccupied or lazy to fully implement the program. This inconsistency in enforcement made the system's impact more sporadic and localized.

The Convent System sparked a profound reaction among the trans women who were consigned to these institutions. They became known as the [[Exconventica]], a term that carries both historical and emotional weight. These women, once imprisoned in the oppressive confines of the convents, emerged to form the backbone of the Luminaran army and provisional government. The oppression they endured served as the spark that unified disparate communities, leading to the organization of a formidable resistance.

The culmination of this resistance was the [[Blooming War]], a conflict that marked a turning point in the history of [[Luminara]]. The war, which saw the [[Exconventica]] and other allies fighting for their rights and freedom, ultimately led to the collapse of the Convent System. The [[Exconventica]] were freed, and many settled in [[Thronaria]], where they continue to rebuild and advocate for their rights.

In the wake of the [[Blooming War]], the legacy of the Convent System remains a somber reminder of the brutalities of religious and social oppression. The memory of the [[Exconventica]] serves as a testament to the resilience and strength of those who have fought against such injustices.
