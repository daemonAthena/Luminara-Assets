The [[Hermit Prince]] is a femboy reporter who travels through [[Luminara]], quietly documenting the state of the people and society. His role is to report back to the princess, advocating for change and assistance. He is also a philosopher who lived through the [[History of the Blooming War|Blooming War]] as a child. He lost his mother in the war. The [[Hermit Prince]] believed in the necessity of a watcher to keep society's powers in check, viewing himself as a gadfly much like Socrates. He is actually bisexual and is prone to fluster because he's also young. He likes to travel around Luminara and observe stuff, and he gets shy around everyone because he has the capacity to be attracted to them.
